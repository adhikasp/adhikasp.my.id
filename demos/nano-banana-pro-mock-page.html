About the store

More than a quick stop.

Sunny Corner Market started as a tiny single-aisle shop and has grown into a neighborhood staple. We're independent, community-focused, and always happy to help you find what you need.

We're a locally owned convenience store that knows most of our customers by name. Whether it's a late-night snack run, a last-minute ingredient, or a cold drink on a hot day, we&aposre here for all the everyday moments.

Our shelves carry a mix of trusted national brands and local favorites from small producers. We keep our layout simple, so you can get in, find what you need, and be on your way in minutes.

Behind the counter is the same family that opened the doors in 2012. We believe in friendly service, fair prices, and making sure everyone who walks in feels welcome.
